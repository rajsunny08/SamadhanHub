Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. Plain-language summary of what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you create an account we store your name, email address and a securely hashed password — never your password in plain text. When you register a problem or post a solution, that content is stored and shown publicly on the site under your name.

Visit counter

We count total site visits and how many people are browsing right now to show on the homepage. This uses a random session id kept in your browser only — no personal data, no cross-site tracking, no cookies sold to anyone.

AI assistant

Messages you send to the Sam chat widget may be sent to our AI provider to generate a reply. Don't share passwords or sensitive personal information in the chat.

Your choices

You can delete any problem you registered at any time from your profile. To delete your account entirely, contact us from the contact page.

Questions about this policy? Email contact@samadhanhub.com.
