HOW IT WORKS

From a stuck moment to a shared solution

SamadhanHub is built on one idea: most problems have already been solved by someone, somewhere. We just need a place to ask.

Register your problem

Describe what's going on, pick a category and urgency, and publish it to the community in under a minute.

People weigh in

Anyone in the community can read your problem and post a solution based on what's worked for them.

Track it to resolved

Move your problem from Open to In Progress to Solved, so others can see what's already been figured out.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Not a forum. Not a help desk.

Forums bury good answers under noise. Help desks close tickets instead of sharing knowledge. SamadhanHub keeps every problem visible with its status and its solutions attached, so the next person with the same problem finds an answer instead of starting over.
